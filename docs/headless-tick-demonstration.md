# CPU-search input replay in TICK

An externally retained recording shows a validated CPU-search input sequence
replayed in the Trackmania client with TICK. The recording shows a
finish time of **17.601 seconds**; the on-car TICK trigram is visually hidden.
The sequence was selected by search and replayed: this recording does not show
an autonomous policy making decisions during the lap, and no training was
performed while recording it.

The recording illustrates client playback. It does not establish synchronized
stepping between TICK and native headless, complete client/headless physical
parity, or performance on other tracks. The result is one selected demonstration,
not a statistical benchmark.

The repository and its source distribution do not include the recording or
screenshots of the game. This description is not a substitute for reviewing
the original recording.
