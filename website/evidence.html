Model implementation details

The article explains the four input groups in plain language. The complete model input specification preserves their order, scaling and clipping, including all 44 road samples and the internal IQN quantile inputs.

Reward implementation details

Forward velocity is clipped to ±100 m/s. Elapsed game time is capped at one second per transition. Every flagged contact step is penalized, without a cooldown. Natural failures include leaving the reference path, getting stuck and exceeding the time limit. Future rewards use a discount factor of 0.995.

The controller disables potential-based progress shaping, demonstration-relative pace shaping, the quadratic speed bonus, the below-target finish bonus and the steering-change penalty.
